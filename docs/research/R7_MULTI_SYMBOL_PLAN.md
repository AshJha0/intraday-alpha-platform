# R7 multi-symbol plan: more symbols on the days we already have (v1.15, DRAFT)

Status: **analysis and plan only.** Nothing was downloaded, ingested,
registered or charged to the ledger. Written 2026-10-11 after the owner
declined Databento (paid tier 2) and approved looking at "more symbols on the
days we already have" (A2 list, `docs/R7_DATA_PLAN.md` §2).

Question: EQ01 @1s, EQ02 @5s and EQ04 @5s were deferred because on the 7
free tier-1 holdout sessions with 3 symbols (AAPL, MSFT, QQQ) their joint
power under the drafted gate is 0.26 / 0.49 / 0.04 (`R7_POWER.md`). Does
pooling the IC over N = 8 or 16 symbols on the same 7 sessions fix that?

**Short answer: not reliably. Recommendation: do not commit to the 16-symbol
ingest for the confirmatory test.** An optional, cheap in-sample-only
measurement of rho (§4, stage 0) is the only step worth taking, and only if
the owner wants to keep these three alphas alive without paid data.

## 1. Model and inputs

Tables: `docs/research/R7_MULTI_SYMBOL_POWER.md`, generated by
`PYTHONPATH=python/src python tools/r7_power.py --multi-symbol`
(`iap.research.r7_power.analyse_multi_symbol`; JSON in
`research/r7/r7_multi_symbol_power.json`; tests in
`python/tests/test_v115_r7_multi_symbol_power.py`).

- Gate unchanged: HAC t >= the ledger Bonferroni threshold (4.432) AND
  deflated Sharpe > 0.95 on the per-session pooled IC series, N = 294 trials,
  S = 7 sessions. The pooled IC over more symbols is still **one** hypothesis
  per alpha, so N does not grow.
- Symbol i, session s: `IC_is = mu + c_s + e_is`, per-symbol session variance
  `sigma_1^2`, cross-symbol correlation `rho` (the common market factor:
  volatility, news, index flow move every symbol's IC on the same day). The
  N-symbol pooled series has variance `sigma_1^2 (rho + (1-rho)/N)`. It is
  calibrated on the measured 3-symbol fold sd, so only the ratio
  `(rho + (1-rho)/N) / (rho + (1-rho)/3)` enters. At N = 16 the sd falls by
  0.73 (rho 0.2), 0.89 (rho 0.5), 0.97 (rho 0.8); even at rho = 0 only by
  0.43. The within-session HAC SE is rescaled the same way (an assumption).
- **The DSR leg is the binding constraint**, not HAC: on 7 sessions it
  needs a per-session Sharpe of the pooled IC of about 3.5. The 3-symbol
  values are EQ01 2.1, EQ02 2.6, EQ04 1.5 (in-sample effect).

**rho cannot be estimated from the committed artefacts.** The in-sample
alpha reports (`data/real/multi7/research/alpha_reports/EQ0x.json`, pinned in
`research/r7/r7_power_inputs.json`) carry the pooled per-fold IC and the
per-symbol IC pooled over all folds, but no symbol x session matrix, so the
common and idiosyncratic parts are not identified (any rho in [0, 1] fits the
numbers). The v1.8 holdout results carry no per-symbol IC. Hence the grid
rho = 0.2 / 0.5 / 0.8. For high-frequency order-flow signals on large caps a
common daily component is expected (shared volatility regime, index
arbitrage through QQQ), so rho near 0 is the optimistic edge, not the centre.

**Per-symbol dispersion is large** (in-sample OOS IC, 2019-20):

| alpha | AAPL | MSFT | QQQ | mean | sd across symbols |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| EQ01 @1s | +0.063 | +0.125 | +0.179 | +0.122 | 0.058 |
| EQ02 @5s | +0.021 | +0.050 | +0.065 | +0.045 | 0.022 |
| EQ04 @5s | +0.015 | +0.022 | +0.003 | +0.014 | 0.009 |

QQQ carries the largest EQ01/EQ02 IC and AAPL the smallest. Thirteen new
single stocks need not look like the 3-symbol mean; if they look like AAPL,
the pooled effect `mu` of the 16-symbol universe falls by a third to a half,
which removes more power than the variance reduction adds. The tables assume
`mu` unchanged, i.e. they are optimistic on this axis.

## 3. Cost and capacity (no execution)

Measured rates (`docs/R7_DATA_PLAN.md` §3 and the committed dataset
manifests): ingest 3.65 min per GB of gzip, dominated by parsing the whole
file (2019-10-30: 286 M of 294 M messages were other symbols), so adding
symbols adds mainly map/spool/normalize time; resident sort ~80 B per event;
peak 7.75 GB RAM measured on the 2-day 2026 holdout ingest; 16 GB machine,
one heavy job at a time.

Per-session events today (3 symbols): 2019-20 days 4.5-8.6 M (AAPL 1.3-2.2 M,
MSFT 1.3-2.3 M, QQQ 1.9-4.9 M); 2026 days 40-46 M (AAPL 4.9-6.7 M, MSFT
4.0-4.1 M, QQQ 32-36 M). New symbols are **not measured**; the estimate below
assumes each new single stock is 1-2x AAPL on the same day (NVDA, TSLA, AMD
likely above that).

**Order (if ever executed):** (1) fix the symbol universe by a rule written
down before any new-symbol data is read (below); (2) re-download and ingest
the in-sample days for the new symbols only; (3) compute in-sample ICs and
the **symbol x session IC matrix, estimate rho**, rerun
`tools/r7_power.py --multi-symbol` with the measured rho and the new pooled
in-sample `mu`; (4) only then write and **sign** a pre-registration fixing
symbols, alphas, horizons, gate and threshold; (5) only then ingest holdout
days for the new symbols. No holdout day is ingested for a new symbol before
(4).

**Survivorship / point-in-time constituents.** The A2 list is QQQ's top 15
by weight in October 2026; choosing it today and applying it to 2019-21 is

listed / not public) on 2019-21 days: PLTR (IPO 2020-09-30, Nasdaq listing
and Nasdaq-100 entry only in late 2024), WMT (NYSE-listed until its move to

Nasdaq point-in-time holdings before use). Nasdaq ITCH carries Nasdaq-venue
orders for every NMS stock, so an NYSE name is present but with a
venue-dependent share of the book. Proposed rule: the universe is QQQ plus
the 15 largest Nasdaq-100 weights **on the first holdout date**
(2025-11-28) that were also Nasdaq-listed and trading on every in-sample
date; GOOG/GOOGL counted once. Results are reported both pooled and
per-symbol, with the 3 original symbols separately.

## 4. Recommendation

**Not worth it** as a route to confirm EQ01/EQ02/EQ04 on the free 7-session
holdout. In the realistic part of the grid (rho >= 0.5, or effects decayed
toward the v1.8 holdout) N = 16 leaves joint power at 0.0-0.6, for roughly
15-20 h of ingest, 200-600 GB of datasets and an external disk. Keep the
three alphas deferred to a larger session sample (paid data or future free
Nasdaq sample days).

Optional **stage 0** (owner's call; no holdout data touched): ingest the 10
in-sample days for the new symbols only (~3 h, fits on disk with the
re-downloaded gzip deleted after ingest), estimate rho and the 16-symbol
pooled `mu`, rerun the tool. Kill criterion, fixed now: proceed to a
pre-registration only if the measured rho (upper end of a bootstrap 80 %
interval over sessions) is <= 0.2 **and** joint power at N = 16 with the
measured `mu` is >= 0.8 for at least one of the three alphas. Ten sessions
give a coarse rho estimate, so expect the interval to be wide; a wide
interval is itself a stop.

No DRAFT pre-registration stub is written: the recommendation is not worth it.
