# v2.2.0 release notes: Round 0 gates, full-market bars, an offline-measured AI layer

Written 2026-11-18 on branch `round0-gates`; the ten-session results were filled in from their
committed artefacts before the merge to `main` (pull request 447, merge commit f149af53,
2026-11-19; [RELEASE_CHECKLIST_v2.2.0.md](RELEASE_CHECKLIST_v2.2.0.md), step 0.2). Every number below
is copied from the file named beside it.

## What this release is

**No alpha is confirmed.** Nothing on `main` is above *development-supported*. This round set the
gates any further study must pass, built a full-market bars path on the free Nasdaq ITCH sample
days, and ran development studies on them; it also made the AI layer measurable offline. The two
free routes to a confirmation sample are closed. The intraday breadth route on IEX HIST closed
because IEX withdrew its historical captures from direct download (the HIST index answers 403 since
2026-11-14; the owner's browser check on 2026-11-18 read "Historical PCAP data is not currently
available for direct download"; [ROUND0_GATES.md](../../research/round0/ROUND0_GATES.md) section 6).
The daily route has no eligible free source: Stooq failed DD1 and the owner dropped Tiingo (R0-4,
same file, section 1). What remains is ten in-sample Nasdaq ITCH sessions, and ten sessions cannot
confirm a realistic Sharpe: they detect, with 80 % power at one registered test, only a net Sharpe of
about 16 or more (ROUND0_GATES.md section 8, from
[PNL_POWER_TABLE.md](../research/PNL_POWER_TABLE.md)); a Sharpe near 1 needs about 1,980 sessions.
The event-driven quoter did not rescue the market-making result: on the one development session it
lost more than the grid quoter it was built to improve.

## Negative and limiting facts, first

- **Event-driven quoter, 2019-01-30, AAPL, MSFT and QQQ, one in-sample session.** Net: grid
  -10,155.22 USD, events -11,201.51 USD. The event quoter's fills are less adversely selected (1 s
  markout -0.37 against -0.45 bps of quote notional) but it gives that back in spread captured (0.11
  against 0.17 bps) and taker fees (847.60 against 57.41 USD), and sent about three times the
  messages ([QUOTER_EVENT_DRIVEN_DEV.md](../../research/round1/QUOTER_EVENT_DRIVEN_DEV.md) sections 5
  and 6). One session, no variance estimate, no claim either way about the average.
- **Breadth on bars, one session (2019-01-30, 500 names).** All four cells are negative net of the
  quoted spread; the primary (VWAP deviation, 1-minute delay) is -8.90 bps per rebalance net, -1.07
  bps gross, against a median round-trip spread cost of 4.29 bps
  ([BREADTH_BARS_20190130.md](../../research/round1/BREADTH_BARS_20190130.md)). The t values there
  are across the rebalances of one session and carry no confirmation weight.
- **Pooled ten-session reversal study.** Net of the quoted spread at the primary 1-minute delay, VWAP deviation -6.24 bps per rebalance (session-level t -11.71, 0 of 10 sessions positive) and residual reversion -6.29 (t -9.33, 0 of 10); at delay 0, -6.33 (t -11.30) and -6.27 (t -8.90); before the spread, +0.66 and +0.59 bps at the primary delay (`research/round1/BREADTH_BARS_POOLED.md`).
- **Families F1 to F3**, pre-specified before the pooled result on the same ten sessions. The rule is K = 8 registered
  primaries, Bonferroni one-sided p below 0.003125 (session-level t above 3.55 on 9 degrees of
  freedom), applied to every candidate; it was K = 7 (p below 0.00357, t above 3.46) until the F3
  erratum added F3 version 2
  ([FAMILIES_PRESPEC_2026-11-18.md](../../research/round1/FAMILIES_PRESPEC_2026-11-18.md) sections 4 and 8):
  F1 intraday momentum net -5.78 bps per rebalance (session-level t -4.71, 1 of 10 sessions positive); F2 opening reversal net -10.57 bps (t -1.97, 2 of 10);
  F3 closing-auction imbalance version 1 had no score on any session (a bar-index error in the specification and a dissemination-start fact; erratum in the pre-specification, no look spent); version 2, registered before any F3 return existed and raising K to 8, net +0.32 bps (t 0.64, 4 of 10), with gross +1.98 bps (t 3.94) and rank IC 0.127 (t 4.53) positive in all ten sessions as diagnostics.
- **Sealed nine-session holdout.** The rule fixed before the pooled result: at most one candidate goes
  forward, and only if it has a positive ten-session mean net, a one-sided p below 0.003125 (0.00357 before
  the F3 erratum) and at least 7 of 10 positive sessions; otherwise the holdout stays sealed
  (FAMILIES_PRESPEC sections 5 and 8;
  [DECISIONS_2026-11-18_overnight.md](../../research/round1/DECISIONS_2026-11-18_overnight.md) row 4).
  Outcome: not taken. No candidate met the rule fixed before the results (positive mean net, one-sided p below 0.003125, at least 7 of 10 sessions positive; `research/round1/BREADTH_FAMILIES.md`), so the nine sessions stay sealed and unread.
  Nine sessions detect only a net Sharpe of about 17 or more (ROUND0_GATES.md section 8).
- **Looks.** The development studies counted 4 looks (breadth, one session) and 6 (quoter); the
  pooled study counts 40 (two scores, two entry delays, ten sessions; the one-session study's 4 are among them) and the families 30 (three families, ten sessions); with the quoter comparison's 6 that is 76 for the round.
  Ledger after entry: the round's 76 entries are in `research/round1/looks_ledger.json` (the same hash-chained format); `research/experiments.json` is unchanged, because its totals feed worked examples computed at that total. Merging the entries and regenerating those examples is an open item (`research/round1/DECISIONS_2026-11-18_overnight.md`, decision 13).
- **Known-null calibration.** The v1.18 A0 FAIL (79 and 80 rejections out of 1,854, 4.3 %) is not
  evidence of over-rejection: about one valid-null world in six shows 79 or more
  ([ROUND0_GATES.md](../../research/round0/ROUND0_GATES.md) section 4, `a0_conditional_rate_sim.json`).
  The recorded verdict stands and gate F3 stays open.
- **AI layer.** No live model eval: under the zero-spend rule no model API is called, so nothing here
  measures a real model. The injection pattern flag detects 16 of 61 attacks; the allowlist is the
  layer that holds ([AI_STACK.md](../AI_STACK.md)).
