## How to read these notes (for every reader)

**The plain idea.** Each sentence that says something about a strategy, a dataset or the platform carries a label
saying how strong the support is. The labels run from strongest to weakest. Almost everything in this release sits at
the weak end, and the negative results come first so they cannot be missed.

**The formal statement.** The seven evidence classes of U0 section 10:

| class | meaning | what the manifest must cite |
|---|---|---|
| independently confirmed | a registered single-shot decision executed under custody separate from the developer | signed prereg, runner log, runner record, custody with a separate approver |
| mechanically isolated | the same, executed by the registered runner under the developer's sole custody | signed prereg, runner log, runner record |
| development-supported | supported on development or validation data, ledgered, never on sealed data | at least one artefact |
| exploratory | a look that suggested something; nothing is claimed from it | at least one artefact |
| rejected | tested and failed, registered or not | the registered set, or artefacts |
| data-ineligible | cannot be tested on the data the programme holds | a reason and an artefact |
| pending | not yet decided | a reason; no evidence cited |

For platform claims (section 7), "development-supported" means the behaviour is shown by tests on synthetic or
development fixtures that CI runs; it says nothing about returns.

**A worked example.** Claim N01 reads "the free daily route fails all five DD1 tests", class **rejected**, citing the
finding record, the ingestion job and its tests. A reader who wants to check it opens the manifest, finds row N01,
re-hashes the three files with `evidence_manifest.py check`, and reads the counts in the finding record.

## What this release is, and is not

**It is** the v2.0 research platform at engineering level A, together with one first-class negative result about the
data. The manifest holds 19 claims: 2 rejected, 4 data-ineligible, 4 pending, 0 exploratory, 9
development-supported, 0 mechanically isolated, 0 independently confirmed.

**It is not** a trading result. No alpha is confirmed. No registered run was executed, so there is no mechanically
isolated result, and no independently confirmed one is possible under sole custody. Levels B, C and D of the plan
(section 9) are not reached. No latency figure is claimed in these notes.

## 1. Rejected results, first

1. **The free daily data route fails DD1.** **rejected** (manifest N01; run 40328224317 on main 7c755bc1;
   [finding record](evidence/DD1_FREE_ROUTE_FINDING_2026-10-26.md); [`tools/ingest/dd1_daily.py`](../../tools/ingest/dd1_daily.py);
   decision DR-19).

   *Plain idea:* the free Stooq prices have lost most of the companies that left the indices, so any backtest on them
   would look mostly at survivors.

   *Formal statement:* all five DD1 tests failed on the development partition: survivorship 315 of 372 index leavers
   missing (85 % against a 2 % threshold), 268 of them absent from the Stooq archive; opens equal to the prior close on
   13.1 % of rows (threshold 5 %); 57 residual split jumps; 323 index members without prices; 2 of 15,356 calendar
   sessions missing against the French factor dates. A no-key check of Tiingo's public ticker list left 101 of the 372
   leavers missing (27 %), so switching vendor would not pass either, and mixing two vendors breaks the one-route
   rule. No test was relaxed.

   *Worked example:* a reversal strategy buys recent losers. If a loser that later delisted is missing from the data,
   its worst weeks never enter the backtest; with 85 % of leavers missing, the measured return would describe the
   recoveries, not the strategy.

2. **PF-3 fails size outside a 1-session holding horizon.** **rejected** (manifest N02;
   [`PF-3_STUDY.json`](../../research/v2/confirmation_evidence/PF-3_STUDY.json),
   [`PF-3_STUDY.md`](../../research/v2/confirmation_evidence/PF-3_STUDY.md); decision DR-17).

   *Plain idea:* the confirmation test for a combined book only behaves correctly when every part of the book is
   rebalanced each session. With longer holdings, one or two years of sessions do not hold enough independent
   observations.

   *Formal statement:* `P-SCBB` is validated for `iid_normal`, `garch11` and `t3` with a 1-session
   overlapping-exposure horizon. Every mixture with a component held 10 or more sessions fails size (k 461 to 1,373
   against the bar of 463) and has 6 to 18 % power at the reference effect. PF-3 revision 1 stands as studied; no
   block length or threshold was re-tuned.
