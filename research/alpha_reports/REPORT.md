# Flagship alpha promotion report (spec §§11-13, §20)

Generated by `research/alpha_reports/run_all.py` on the bundled 2-day
seeded synthetic dataset (19 instruments, data/features/). Every metric
is deterministic — an identical rerun reproduces every number in every
table; only this runtime line and the appending experiments ledger
(hence the multiple-testing counts) move. Runtime 105s.

**Honesty note (spec §32):** the data is synthetic and strongly
mean-reverting; several textbook microstructure hypotheses do not hold on
it. Alphas failing their gates below are reported truthfully — a REJECT
on synthetic data is a statement about this dataset, not the idea.
`hyp` = fitted slope agrees with the stated economic rationale's sign;
alphas with `hyp=no` can never be PROMOTE regardless of |IC|.

**Currency (round 3, PLATFORM_CONVENTIONS.md §11.6):** every P&L, cost
and capital figure below is in USD. FX instruments are quoted in their
quote currency (JPY for USD/JPY, CAD for USD/CAD, CHF for USD/CHF, GBP
for EUR/GBP) and each row's P&L is converted at the prevailing mid of
the conversion pair from the same frame set; earlier reports summed
quote-currency P&L as if USD (JPY-dominated FX rows) — those numbers
were wrong in scale, not in sign, and are superseded here.

## Pinned promotion gates (spec §20)

- PROMOTE: leakage pass AND gate IC >= 0.01
  AND gate t >= 4.365 AND fold sign consistency >= 0.7 AND >= 3 non-degenerate folds AND hypothesis confirmed AND net P&L > 0 at 1x costs.
- ITERATE: leakage pass AND gate IC >= 0.005 AND gate t >= 1.5.
- REJECT: otherwise (always, on leakage failure).

## Methods (`v2`, iap.validation.methods)

The defaults since v1.5.0; each has a named legacy rule
(`run_all.py --methods legacy_v1` reproduces the v1.4.0 report).

- **Rows every IC scores:** a valid label, or one invalid for BLACKOUT alone scored at its realised reopen return.
- **Gate IC:** the pooled IC of the standardized signal on those rows, uncrossed book only.
- **Gate t:** the HAC t of the pooled slope (the significance of the gate IC itself); lag count `L` follows the horizon.
- **PROMOTE t threshold:** 4.365 = the Bonferroni |t| at the run's gate look count, 3,936 (the ledger before this run plus the 2,016 this run adds; never below 3.0).
- **Backtest:** cost-aware positions (enter only when |expected return| exceeds the round-trip spread + fee, hold for the label horizon or until an opposite signal that clears the same bar, renew at half of it), fills capped at the displayed L1 size, only the rows the IC scores are traded, square-root impact. An alpha whose forecast never clears its costs makes NO trade: its net P&L is 0, which does not pass `net P&L > 0`.
- **Capacity:** edge breakeven — the size at which the realised gross edge per round trip of the 1x backtest equals its cost, capped at the participation line; 0 for an alpha that does not trade or loses before costs.

- **crossed book** is the fraction of emissions whose CONSOLIDATED
  book is crossed (`spread_ticks_v1 < 0`) because one venue's quote
  is stale. On the FX pairs this is 19-37 % of rows (mean 30 %), and the mid on
  those rows reverts mechanically when the stale LP refreshes — a
  vol-scaled momentum signal is paid for measuring that artefact.
  Every IC in this report is therefore split crossed/uncrossed.
- **mean row gap** is the spacing of emissions. Equity rows are ~3.1-3.3 s
  apart, FX rows ~21.7-22.1 s: any "+1 event" latency claim means two
  different things, which is why the latency stress is in event time.
- **zero 1s / zero 1m** is the fraction of VALID labels that are
  exactly 0. A 500 ms-5 s FX label is 89-99 % zeros: a REJECT at those
  horizons is a sampling artefact of the emission cadence, not
  evidence against the hypothesis.
- **label max age** is the pinned freshness bound
  `max(5 s, 2 x median quote gap)`: a label whose forward mid is
  older than this is INVALID (API_FEATURES section 6). Equity rows
  are sparse relative to the 5 s floor, so the bound removes a
  material share of the equity labels at horizons of 10 s and more
  (`valid 1m` above); 15 m labels anchored in the last 15 minutes of
  a session do not exist at all.

## Master table (walk-forward OOS, expanding purged+embargoed folds)

Folds are quantiles of the ROW INDEX, not of the wall span: the equity
session is 6.5 h of each 24 h day, so equal wall segments leave folds
EMPTY or badly unequal while a report still claims 4 folds. A fold
with fewer than 32 usable pairs is DEGENERATE and counts as a failed
fold in `folds+`; PROMOTE needs >= 3 non-degenerate folds.

`IC` is the pooled OOS IC on the standardized signal; `gate IC` is the
same IC restricted to rows whose consolidated book was NOT crossed
(a crossed book means a stale venue quote whose mid mechanically
reverts). **The gates read `gate IC` and `gate t`**; `t other` is the
t-statistic the gate does not read (within-bucket under the default
methods, pooled-slope under the legacy ones). `fails` lists the PROMOTE
gates the alpha does not pass.
