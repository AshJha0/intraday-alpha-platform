# Flagship alpha promotion report (spec §§11-13, §20)

Generated by `research/alpha_reports/run_all.py` on the bundled 2-day
seeded synthetic dataset (19 instruments, data/features/). Every metric
is deterministic — an identical rerun reproduces every number in every
table; only this runtime line and the appending experiments ledger
(hence the multiple-testing counts) move. Runtime 32s.

**Honesty note (spec §32):** the data is synthetic and strongly
mean-reverting; several textbook microstructure hypotheses do not hold on
it. Alphas failing their gates below are reported truthfully — a REJECT
on synthetic data is a statement about this dataset, not the idea.
`hyp` = fitted slope agrees with the stated economic rationale's sign;
alphas with `hyp=no` can never be PROMOTE regardless of |IC|.

**Currency (round 3, PLATFORM_CONVENTIONS.md §11.6):** every P&L, cost
and capital figure below is in USD. FX instruments are quoted in their
quote currency (JPY for USD/JPY, CAD for USD/CAD, CHF for USD/CHF, GBP
for EUR/GBP) and each row's P&L is converted at the prevailing mid of
the conversion pair from the same frame set; earlier reports summed
quote-currency P&L as if USD (JPY-dominated FX rows) — those numbers
were wrong in scale, not in sign, and are superseded here.

## Pinned promotion gates (spec §20)

- PROMOTE: leakage pass AND UNCROSSED OOS IC >= 0.01
  AND its NW t >= 3.0 AND fold sign consistency >= 0.7 AND >= 3 non-degenerate folds AND hypothesis confirmed AND net P&L > 0 at 1x costs.
- ITERATE: leakage pass AND OOS IC >= 0.005 AND NW t >= 1.5.
- REJECT: otherwise (always, on leakage failure).

- **crossed book** is the fraction of emissions whose CONSOLIDATED
  book is crossed (`spread_ticks_v1 < 0`) because one venue's quote
  is stale. On the FX pairs this is 19-37 % of rows (mean 30 %), and the mid on
  those rows reverts mechanically when the stale LP refreshes — a
  vol-scaled momentum signal is paid for measuring that artefact.
  Every IC in this report is therefore split crossed/uncrossed.
- **mean row gap** is the spacing of emissions. Equity rows are ~3.3 s
  apart, FX rows ~15-22 s: any "+1 event" latency claim means two
  different things, which is why the latency stress is in event time.
- **zero 1s / zero 1m** is the fraction of VALID labels that are
  exactly 0. A 500 ms-5 s FX label is 89-99 % zeros: a REJECT at those
  horizons is a sampling artefact of the emission cadence, not
  evidence against the hypothesis.
- **label max age** is the pinned freshness bound
  `max(5 s, 2 x median quote gap)`: a label whose forward mid is
  older than this is INVALID (API_FEATURES section 6), which is why
  15 m equity labels near the session close no longer exist.

occupies ~2.6 h of each 24 h day, so equal wall segments used to leave
two of four folds EMPTY while the report still claimed 4 folds. A fold
with fewer than 32 usable pairs is DEGENERATE and counts as a failed
fold in `folds+`; PROMOTE needs >= 3 non-degenerate folds.

same IC restricted to rows whose consolidated book was NOT crossed
(a crossed book means a stale venue quote whose mid mechanically

## Multiple testing

Multiple testing: 1068 experiments recorded. Bonferroni per-test p-threshold 4.68e-05 (|t| >= 4.07); deflated-Sharpe-style selection yardstick: expected max |t| under the global null is ~3.73 — any t below that is consistent with pure selection over this many trials.

`ledger_n_at_report` = **1068** (70 distinct configurations). This number is
read from `research/experiments.json` at render time, so the report and
the ledger can never disagree — the stale "1 224 experiments" note
earlier versions carried is gone.

Every walk-forward evaluation, decay-curve horizon, stress variant and
backtest in this run is counted in `research/experiments.json` (28 looks per alpha per run), plus the one-time design horizon scan (216 looks). Experiments are DE-DUPLICATED by (alpha, kind, configuration): re-running this script does not inflate the denominator, so the selection-adjusted threshold is a property of the research design, not of how often the script ran.
