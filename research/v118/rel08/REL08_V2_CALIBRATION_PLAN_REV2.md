## 2. Checkpoint (non-decisional)

`checkpoint_100_NONDECISIONAL.md` is written after the first 100 replications of each gating cell (the same table as the final report, restricted to r = 0..99). It carries the
label "NON-DECISIONAL checkpoint; never used to stop, extend or tune". It exists to give the reviewer and the coordinator early engineering information (does the run behave
as designed, are rates anywhere near the bounds). Nothing in the run reads it. The final report contains the 200-replication tables and may display the first-100 tables as a
separately labelled section; the verdict uses only the 200.

## 3. Acceptance criterion for the confirmatory decision (unchanged from the original plan, fixed sample R = 200)

* **A.** In each of the six gating cells, family-level one-sided 95 % Clopper-Pearson upper bound <= 5 % (at most 4 family events in 200).
* **B.** In each gating cell, per-variant pooled C3 false-pass rate upper bound <= 1.43 % over 1,400 variant-screens (at most 12 events).
* **C.** Pooled over the six gating cells the family point estimate <= 5 %.
* **D.** No computing failure; 200 completed replications in each gating cell.

PASS = A, B, C and D; otherwise FAIL (no tuning follows a FAIL; a different inference needs a new plan). N4 and the power check are never part of the verdict.

## 4. Three levels of evidence, reported separately

**(a) Observed calibration (descriptive).** For every null cell: replications, family false-continuation events, per-variant continuation and C3-pass counts, point
estimates and Clopper-Pearson bounds (one-sided and two-sided), the replication-level bootstrap interval for the per-variant rate, the share of screens
`underpowered` by coverage/C1/C2/C4, and the bootstrap SE versus empirical SD diagnostics. This is a description of what the generators produced; it carries no
verdict.

**(b) Decision-level evidence (the prospectively declared rule).** Whether the six-cell gating family satisfies criteria A-D of section 3. **Caveat stated in advance:** the six
cell-level bounds are individual 95 % bounds, not a simultaneous 95 % guarantee over the six cells. The report therefore also shows, as information only (not a gate), the
upper bound of each cell at the Bonferroni-adjusted level 1 - 0.05/6, and the number of the six cells within 1 event of the limit. A PASS means only that this
specific rule was met; it is not a statement that the true false-continuation rate is below 5 % in every cell with 95 % simultaneous confidence.

**(c) Inferential limitations (what no outcome supports).** No claim of nominal 5 % family-wise error control; no day-level generalisation (the six sessions are fixed and the
inference is conditional on them); no real-market-data claim (four synthetic generators, no real microstructure, no data holes); no trading edge, no promotion. A FAIL
blocks signing of v2 with this inference; a PASS does not authorise signing, real-data execution or promotion (those need the reviewer, the F3 status (OPEN) and the identity-bound signature
protocol). The power-side check, if it shows low power, is reported as low power and not as a defect to repair by tuning.

## 5. Staged-run integrity (why this is a fixed-sample decision)

The decision depends only on the completed 200 per gating cell; the run does not branch on any interim number (`run_calibration.py all` contains no conditional on results,
which a reader can verify). The reviewer's cautions about optional continuation (stopping at 100 on a favourable result, extending on an unfavourable one) therefore do not
apply: there is no stopping and no optional extension. The first-100 outputs are a subset of the 200 and are labelled accordingly.

## 6. Short-circuit evidence (decision-equivalence)

Optional flag `short_circuit` (`iap.research.rel08_screen_v2`): when C1, C2, C4 or C3 already settles `underpowered` or `stop`, the two books behind C6 and C7 are not run
(their two inputs are NaN, `robustness_books_computed = false`). Evidence:

* `python/tests/test_v118_rel08_screen_v2.py::test_short_circuit_...`: full versus fast, null and strong planted effect;
* `python/tests/test_v118_rel08_v2_short_circuit.py`: **exhaustive over all 256 criteria vectors** (outcome independent of C5-C8 unless C1-C4 hold and C3 passes); **threshold-boundary
  cases** (C2, C3 net, C3 lower bound and C4 thresholds set exactly at the observed statistic and one epsilon either side); **solver-fallback cases** (the v1 setting where the optimiser holds a
  flat book, and a binding trading cap); an end-to-end planted-edge case;
* `calibration/equivalence_check.py` and `equivalence.json`: 10 synthetic screens, 70 variant-screens, seeds 20269300+: identical in every statistic, criterion, outcome and reason;
  58 short-circuited, 12 reached the C6/C7 books, 21 C3 passes and 10 continues (edge cases covered).

What the plan reports does not use C6/C7 values; the per-record C6/C7 flags of a non-continuing variant read False and are not used anywhere in the tables.
