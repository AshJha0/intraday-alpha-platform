# Planted-signal power study

Generated by `python -m iap.research power` (`iap.research.power`, report version 3). Do not edit by hand.

How often does the validation chain (`iap.validation.validate_alpha`, the pinned research execution model under the `v2` method bundle) detect an effect of known size planted into the synthetic generator, on how many sessions, and how often does it report one that is not there?

## Design

- Generator config: `research/power/generator_planted.json` (base seed 20261003); universe SYN.EQ.001, SYN.EQ.002, SYN.ETF.IDX.
- Reference effect (level 1.0): informed order flow `strength=0.4`, kernel decay 0.9 over 20 one-second steps; ETF lead-lag `beta=0.4` at a lag of 2 step(s). A level scales both; the reference is fixed and is not tuned to this report.
- Levels: 0, 0.5, 1 (0 = null); 20 generator seeds per cell; every rate is `detections/runs [Wilson 95 % interval]`.
- Sessions: each run is generated at 4 sessions and evaluated on its first 1, 2, 4 (the first n sessions of a run are exactly the n-session run of the same seed).
- Scenarios: `stable` = the effect holds for the whole sample; `break` = it is reversed from the middle of the sample on (levels 1, 4 sessions).
- Detectors: `lead_lag:EQ10@1s` (declared horizon); `lead_lag:EQ10@5s` (matched horizon); `order_flow:EQ04@5s` (declared horizon); `order_flow:EQ04@10s` (matched horizon). The matched horizon is the shortest pinned label horizon that covers the planted mechanism — a rule on the planted block, not on the results.
- Thresholds on the pooled-slope HAC t (gate IC > 0 required): `fixed` 3; `gate` 4.365, the PROMOTE threshold in force (Bonferroni at the 3,936 looks the committed promotion reports were judged at); `study` 4.164, Bonferroni over this study's own 1,600 tests. Verdicts are computed at 4.365, the largest of the three.

## Minimum detectable effect and sessions needed

Fit of `t ~ N((kappa0 + kappa * level) * sqrt(sessions), sd)` to the chain's t of every stable run, the null included (`kappa` = the t the reference effect adds on one session; `kappa0` = the t of the null on one session — where it is negative the generator's own microstructure works against the planted effect, which has to overcome it first). MDE = the level detected with 80% probability; `sessions for reference` = sessions at which level 1 reaches 80%. A session count above 4 is an extrapolation under the sqrt law, not a measurement. The model is linear in the level; a mechanism whose response flattens with size (the lead-lag correlation is `beta / sqrt(1 + beta^2)`) is understated at level 1 when larger levels are in the fit, and the measured rate above is then the better number.

## Reading it

- `at gate` is the significance leg of PROMOTE as the platform applies it; `promote` additionally needs fold sign consistency, a confirmed hypothesis sign and net P&L > 0 at 1x costs. `evidence` = verdict ITERATE or PROMOTE.
- `trades at 1x` is the mean number of trades the cost-aware backtest makes on the last fold: an alpha whose forecast never clears its round-trip cost makes none, and its net P&L of 0 does not pass the cost gate. A detected effect that is too small to trade is still not a PROMOTE — that is the cost gate working, not a loss of power.
- Session prefixes of one run share data, so the rows of one detector at different session counts are not independent of each other; the 20 runs within a row are. The recompute leakage probe (first seed of every cell, full session count) passed wherever it ran.
- `research/power/POWER_REPORT.md` is the default grid, sized for the CI `regenerate` job; `research/power/extended/POWER_REPORT.md` is the larger one (`python -m iap.research power --sessions 1,2,4,8 --levels 0,0.5,1,2 --power-out-dir ../research/power/extended`, the opt-in `power_extended` step of `tools/regenerate_dataset_artifacts.py`). Any other grid runs the same way: `--seeds`, `--sessions`, `--levels`, `--break-levels`, `--jobs`.

Per-run numbers are in `POWER_REPORT.json`.
