# Planted-signal power study

Generated by `python -m iap.research power` (`iap.research.power`, report version 2). Do not edit by hand.

How often does the validation chain (`iap.validation.validate_alpha`, the pinned research execution model under the `v2` method bundle) detect an effect of known size planted into the synthetic generator, and how often does it report one that is not there?

## Design

- Generator config: `research/power/generator_planted.json` (base seed 20261003); universe SYN.EQ.001, SYN.EQ.002, SYN.ETF.IDX.
- Reference effect (level 1.0): informed order flow `strength=0.4`, kernel decay 0.9 over 20 one-second steps; ETF lead-lag `beta=0.4` at a lag of 2 step(s). A level scales both.
- Levels: 0, 0.5, 1, 2 (0 = null); 3 generator seeds per cell (850875211, 1197896597, 462592942).
- Scenarios: `stable` = the effect holds for the whole sample; `break` = it is reversed from the middle of the sample on.
- Detectors: lead_lag -> EQ10, order_flow -> EQ04.
- Significance: gate IC > 0 and t >= 3. `pooled` is the pooled-slope HAC t (what the PROMOTE gate reads); `within` is the Newey-West t of within-bucket ICs (the legacy gate statistic); `ledger` is the pooled-slope t against the multiple-testing threshold of this study's own 42 tests (t >= 3.24) — the significance gate as PROMOTE applies it.
- `P&L CI > 0`: the 95 % stationary-bootstrap interval (300 resamples) of the pooled walk-forward net P&L at 1x costs lies above zero.

## Reading it

- Level 0 is the false-positive row: nothing is planted, so every non-zero rate there is a false detection.
- The `stable` rows are the power of the chain at each effect size.
- `sig (pooled)` against `sig (within)` shows what the gate statistic changes: the pooled t keeps between-bucket signal the within-bucket t discards. `sig (ledger)` charges the study for the number of things it tried.
- The `break` rows plant an effect that reverses mid-sample. A chain that reports `evidence` there is averaging over a regime change; fold sign consistency is the statistic that should fall.
- With 3 seeds a rate moves in steps of 0.33: this calibrates the chain, it is not a precise power curve.

Per-run numbers are in `POWER_REPORT.json`.
