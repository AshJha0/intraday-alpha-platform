# v1.16.0 release notes (2026-10-15)

## Theme: monetisation

v1.15 confirmed that EQ10@1s and EQ05@1s predict on unseen sessions, but net P&L at 1x cost was 0.
v1.16 asked whether that edge can be turned into money. It made execution costs realistic first, then
tested a cost-aware taker strategy in-sample, and planned to buy fresh data only if something cleared
costs.

**The answer: neither taking nor making pays.** The edge is about 0.1–0.9 bp, and crossing the
spread costs 0.8–2.4 bp. Two-sided skewed quoting loses too, and so does AI4 DeepLOB in-sample. The signals are useful as execution inputs, not as standalone strategies, so v1.17 turns to execution quality and breadth.

## What shipped

### P0: realistic execution (items 1–5)
- **1. Parent-order impact** (opt-in rule 6b, `ExecConfig.impact_model = "parent"`). Temporary impact is
  charged on the parent's cumulative taker qty, plus a permanent leg, so splitting a parent cannot lower
  its cost. The default `"child"` keeps every golden unchanged.
- **2. Impact calibration** (`estimate_calibration(..., impact_fit=True)`). Fits the residual mid move
  after the depth walk against participation as a % of tape ADV: linear, square-root and log-log fits,
  with SEs.
- **3. Prereg realism keys** (`configs/prereg/v3.json`, owner-approved 2026-10-14): venue latency with a
  tail, a latency stress grid, fee tier with sensitivity, exec prints and parent impact.
- **4. SOR opt-ins** (`iap.execution.sor_v2`): sweeps ranked by all-in price, and passive routing that
  uses a queue-hazard fill probability. Deduplicated onto the v1.12 router (#135).
- **5. Algos**: exact POV (`pov_exact`) with full catch-up, and an Almgren-Chriss `eta` taken from the
  residual fit.

### Studies
- **P0-6 in-sample monetisation study (EQ10/EQ05): NO-GO.** The grid was 480 cells (hold × band ×
  signal × sizing × cost × latency) on 10 in-sample sessions. **No cell traded, in any session.** The
  largest expected return was ~0.1–0.2 bp, against a median round-trip cost of ~0.8–1.2 bp
  (`research/v116/MONETISATION_P0-6.md`, #138).
- **Item 7 taker capacity curve: no capacity.** **0 of 45 cells** have a break-even size above zero.
  Tail edges are 0.05–0.4 bp (best 0.92 bp: EQ10, AAPL, 1m, top 0.1%) and the round-trip cost is
  0.8–2.4 bp, so the net at 100 shares is −1.4 to −3.1 bp (`research/v116/CAPACITY_CURVE.md`, #149).
- **M5 two-sided quoting study v2: NO QUOTING EDGE.** It was registered and signed on 2026-10-14 (board seq 16-19) and run in-sample on 6 test sessions. The validity gate passed for every skewed cell: the skew changed 40-49% of quotes. Every cell loses $5.0-6.9k per session, with Bonferroni CIs below 0, and adverse selection is about 4x the spread captured. Skewing with EQ01 or EQ05 cuts the loss by about $750-920 per session against the no-skew control (significant), so the signals are useful as an execution input. The owner recorded the outcomes as rejected (seq 20-23). See `research/quoting_real/QUOTING_V2_RESULT.md` (#157, #158).
- **AI4 DeepLOB (item 10): CLOSED, in-sample failure, holdout not used.** The real-data adapter and streaming trainer were built (`iap.models.lob_real`, `lob_train`). On validation, DeepLOB nets -0.69 bp per trade (gross +0.27 bp against a 0.97 bp cost); the linear baseline nets -0.74 bp and GBM -0.68 bp. The registered accept rule fails on all three symbols. By owner decision (2026-10-15) no holdout look was spent; any future test needs a new pre-registration. See `research/r7/AI4_CLOSED_v116.md`.

### Paper-trade infrastructure (item 9)
- `python -m iap.paper.daily (--date D | --pending)` runs the MVP per session with v3 realism and the
  EOD flatten, then compares live TCA with the backtest. R7 holdout sessions are refused. **Nothing is
  scheduled**: the owner registers the task (docs/runbooks/RUNBOOK_paper_trade.md). The job only
  matters once a rule passes a net-of-cost gate (#144).
